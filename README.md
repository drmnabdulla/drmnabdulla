# Dr. Mostafa Naguib Abdulla

**Semiconductor Technologist & Engineering Leader | Systems Architecture, Design & Integration**

I bring a system-level perspective to semiconductor development, connecting technology choices, system architecture, circuit and block design, and integration with product requirements. My work brings together technical judgment, engineering management, and cross-functional leadership to move ideas toward practical products.

My background includes more than 25 years in semiconductor engineering and leadership, with roles at Intel, Qualcomm, Keyssa, Micron, and Numonyx. I have led multidisciplinary, globally distributed engineering teams and served as chair of JEDEC JC64.2, contributing to industry collaboration and standards development.

Here I share books, research, and practical frameworks for engineers, researchers, and technology leaders. These works reflect different aspects of my experience across semiconductor systems, design, integration, and technology development.

## Technology and leadership perspective

- **Systems and architecture:** connecting product requirements with architecture choices and tradeoffs across semiconductor building blocks.
- **Circuit and block design:** experience spanning high-speed interfaces, memory systems, signal integrity, and analog/mixed-signal and RF engineering.
- **Integration and emerging technologies:** interests in chiplets, heterogeneous integration, advanced packaging, and the interaction between silicon and system design.
- **Engineering leadership:** guiding multidisciplinary teams, technical execution, technology roadmaps, and collaboration across organizational boundaries.
- **Innovation and industry development:** connecting research, standards, technology readiness, and product opportunities.
